# Code of Conduct

This library is for everyone on Earth, of every country, culture, religion, politics, age, gender, ability and background. The people building it should be just as welcoming.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Criticize facts, not people. "This dose doesn't match the WHO guideline (source)" is useful. "You clearly don't know medicine" is not.
- Accept corrections with grace. Everyone here shares the same goal: getting it right.
- Keep content neutral and practical. This is not a place for political, religious or national arguments.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats or sexual content of any kind.
- Deliberately adding false or dangerous information.
- Content that helps people harm others, such as weapons manufacturing, explosives, or instructions for violence.
- Promoting products, services or ideologies.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits and contributions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through the email on their GitHub profiles, or open an issue asking for a private contact.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
