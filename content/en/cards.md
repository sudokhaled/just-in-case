## CPR: not breathing

1. **No response and not breathing normally?** Gasping is not breathing. Call 112, 911 or your local number.
2. **Push hard and fast** in the center of the chest: 5-6 cm (2-2.4 in) deep, 100-120 times a minute. Let the chest rise each time.
3. **If trained,** give 2 breaths after every 30 pushes. If not, push without stopping.
4. **Use a defibrillator (AED)** as soon as one arrives. Do what it says.

**Children, babies and drowning:** give 5 breaths first. Baby: 2 thumbs, 4 cm (1.5 in) deep.

![1 Heel of hand, center of chest. 2 Arms straight, shoulders above hands. 3 Push down 5-6 cm (2-2.4 in).](../../images/cpr-adult.svg)

See [CPR: When Someone Is Not Breathing](../medical/cpr.md)

## Choking

1. **Can they cough?** Tell them to keep coughing.
2. **Cannot speak, cough or breathe:** give 5 hard **back blows** between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.
3. Then 5 **abdominal thrusts:** fist just above the navel, pull sharply in and up.
4. Repeat 5 and 5 until it comes out.

![1 Back blows between the shoulder blades. 2 Fist above the navel, pull in and up.](../../images/choking-adult.svg)

**Baby under 1 year:** 5 back blows face down on your forearm, then 5 chest thrusts face up with the heel of one hand. **Never** abdominal thrusts.

**They collapse:** start CPR.

See [Choking](../medical/choking.md)

## Severe bleeding

1. **Press hard** on the wound with cloth or your bare hands. Lean your weight on it.
2. Keep pressing **10 minutes** without lifting to look.
3. **Deep wound in neck, armpit or groin:** stuff cloth into the wound, then press.
4. **Arm or leg still bleeding:** tourniquet **5-7 cm (2-3 in) above** the wound, not on a joint. Tighten until bleeding stops. **Write the time.**
5. **Never loosen** a tourniquet.
6. Lay them down and keep them warm.

![1 Wound. 2 Tourniquet 5-7 cm (2-3 in) above it, not on the knee. 3 Twisting rod. 4 High and tight if you cannot see the wound.](../../images/tourniquet.svg)

See [Stopping Severe Bleeding](../medical/severe-bleeding.md)

## Recovery position

For someone who **is breathing** but does not respond.

1. Arm nearest you out at a right angle, palm up.
2. Far hand against their cheek. Pull the far knee up.
3. Roll them toward you onto their side.
4. Top leg bent at hip and knee so they cannot roll over.
5. Tilt the head back, mouth pointing slightly down.
6. **Check breathing every minute.** If it stops, roll them onto their back and start CPR.

![1 Head tilted back, mouth down. 2 Hand under the cheek. 3 Top knee bent at a right angle.](../../images/recovery-position.svg)

See [First Aid Basics: Check, Call, Care](../medical/first-aid-basics.md)

## Burns

1. **Stop the burning.** Remove rings and watches.
2. **Cool running water for 20 minutes.** Still useful up to 3 hours later. Never ice, butter, oil or toothpaste.
3. **Cover loosely** with cling film or a clean plastic bag.
4. **Keep the person warm.**

**Get help** for: large burns, burns on the face, hands, feet, genitals or joints, burns all the way around a limb, electrical or chemical burns, smoke breathed in, and burns in babies or older people.

See [Burns](../medical/burns.md)

## Diarrhea: oral rehydration drink

**1 liter (1 quart) clean water + 6 level teaspoons sugar + 1/2 level teaspoon salt.** No saltier than tears.

![1 liter of water, 6 level teaspoons of sugar, half a level teaspoon of salt.](../../images/ors-recipe.svg)

| Age | After each loose stool |
|---|---|
| Under 2 years | 50-100 mL (1/4-1/2 cup) |
| 2-10 years | 100-200 mL (1/2-1 cup) |
| Older | As much as they want |

Small sips. Keep breastfeeding and eating.

**Get help:** very sleepy, cannot drink, blood in the stool, no urine for 6 hours in a baby.

See [Dehydration, Diarrhea and Oral Rehydration](../medical/dehydration.md)

## Safe drinking water

1. **Cloudy?** Let it settle 30 minutes. Pour through folded cloth.
2. **Then one of these:**
   - **Boil** 1 minute (3 minutes above 2,000 m or 6,500 ft).
   - **Bleach** 5-6%, plain: 2 drops per liter. Double if cloudy. Wait 30 minutes.
   - **Sun:** clear plastic bottles, 6 hours in full sun (2 days if cloudy).
3. **Store covered.** Pour it out. Never dip hands or cups in.

None of these remove fuel or chemicals.

See [Making Water Safe to Drink](../water/purify-water.md)

## Carbon monoxide

**Never** run a generator, grill, charcoal, camp stove or car indoors, in a garage or in a tent, not even with a window open.

- Generator: outside, at least **6 m (20 ft)** from doors and windows.
- **Signs:** headache, dizziness, sickness, confusion. Several people or pets ill at once.
- **Act:** get everyone into fresh air now. Call for help. Do not go back in.

See [Carbon Monoxide and Smoke: The Silent Killers](../fire/carbon-monoxide.md)

## Too cold: hypothermia

**Signs:** shivering, clumsy hands, slurred speech, confusion. **Shivering stops = severe.**

1. Out of wind and wet. Remove wet clothes.
2. Insulate **underneath** and all around. Cover the head.
3. Wrap in a blanket and a plastic sheet.
4. Heat packs or warm bottles on the chest and armpits.
5. Warm sweet drinks only if fully awake.
6. **Handle gently.** No alcohol. No rubbing.

See [Hypothermia and Frostbite](../medical/hypothermia-frostbite.md)

## Too hot: heat stroke

**Hot and confused, strange or collapsed = heat stroke. Cool first, then get help.**

1. **Best:** put them in cold water up to the neck.
2. **Or:** soak them with water and fan hard. Ice or cold packs on the neck, armpits and groin.
3. Stop when they shiver or their mind clears.
4. Drinks only if fully awake.

**Prevent it:** shade, water, rest in the hottest hours. Never leave a child in a car.

See [Heat Exhaustion and Heat Stroke](../medical/heat-illness.md)

## Severe allergic reaction

**Signs:** sudden trouble breathing, swollen face, lips or throat, rash, collapse. Often after food, a sting or medicine.

1. **Inject adrenaline** (auto-injector) into the outer thigh, through clothing.
2. Call for help.
3. Lie down with legs raised. If breathing is hard, sit up.
4. **No better after 5 minutes:** second dose.
5. Watch for 24 hours. It can come back.

See [Severe Allergic Reactions (Anaphylaxis)](../medical/allergic-reactions.md)

## Signals for rescue

- **3 of anything means help:** 3 fires, 3 whistle blasts, 3 flashes.
- **SOS:** ... --- ... (short short short, long long long, short short short).
- **Stay with your vehicle** if it is safe. It is easier to see.
- A text message may get through when calls do not.

![Ground symbols at least 3 m (10 ft) long: 1 V, we need help. 2 X, we need medical help. 3 N, no. 4 Y, yes. 5 Arrow, we went this way.](../../images/ground-air-signals.svg)

See [Signaling for Rescue](../navigation/signal-rescue.md)

## Earthquake

1. **Drop** to hands and knees.
2. **Cover** your head and neck under a sturdy table.
3. **Hold on** until the shaking stops.

![1 Drop. 2 Cover your head and neck under a table. 3 Hold on to the table leg.](../../images/drop-cover-hold.svg)

Do not run outside during shaking.

**Near the coast,** after strong or long shaking: go to high ground **now**. A tsunami can arrive in minutes.

**After:** expect aftershocks. Smell gas? Leave. No flames or switches. Trapped? Tap on pipes. Cover your mouth.

See [Earthquakes](../disasters/earthquake.md)

## Flood

**Turn around, do not drown.**

- **15 cm (6 in)** of moving water can knock you down.
- **30 cm (1 ft)** can float a car.
- **60 cm (2 ft)** sweeps most vehicles away.

1. Go to higher ground at once.
2. Never walk, drive or swim through flood water.
3. Stay at least 10 m (33 ft) from fallen power lines.
4. Flood water is sewage. Clean every wound. Throw out food it touched.

See [Floods](../disasters/flood.md)

- Water and food for 3 days
- Medicines, glasses, a first aid kit
- Copies of documents, some cash
- Phone, charger, power bank
- Flashlight, radio, spare batteries
