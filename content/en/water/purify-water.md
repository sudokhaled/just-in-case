> [!CAUTION]
> **Clear water is not safe water.** Most deadly germs are invisible. Treat all water from rivers, lakes, wells, rain tanks and broken pipes before you drink it, cook with it, brush your teeth with it or wash wounds with it.
>
> **Boiling and bleach do not remove chemicals.** Water that smells of fuel, has an oily film, or comes from near mines, factories or floodwater must not be used, even after treatment, unless you have no other source at all.

## Quick steps

1. **Clear it.** If the water is cloudy, let it stand for 30 minutes, then pour the clear top part through a clean cloth folded several times.
2. **Kill the germs.** Use any one method:
   - **Boil:** bring to a rolling boil for **1 minute**. Above 2,000 m (6,500 ft), boil for **3 minutes**.
   - **Bleach:** add **2 drops of plain household bleach per liter (quart)**. Use 4 drops if the water is cloudy or very cold. Stir and wait **30 minutes**.
   - **Sunlight (SODIS):** fill clear plastic bottles, lay them in full sun for **6 hours** (2 days if the sky is mostly cloudy).
3. **Store it clean.** Keep treated water covered, in a clean container. Pour it out. Never dip hands or cups into it.

## Which method to use

| Method | Kills bacteria | Kills viruses | Kills parasites (giardia, crypto) | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Boiling | Yes | Yes | Yes | Fuel, a pot |
| Bleach (chlorine) | Yes | Yes | Giardia yes, crypto **no** | Bleach, a dropper or spoon |
| Iodine | Yes | Yes | Giardia yes, crypto **no** | Iodine tincture or tablets |
| Sunlight (SODIS) | Yes | Most | Most | Clear plastic bottles, strong sun |
| Good filter (0.1-0.2 micron) | Yes | **No** | Yes | A commercial filter |
| Cloth or sand filter | Partly | **No** | Partly | Cloth, sand, charcoal |

**Best combination:** filter (any kind) to clear the water, then boil or chlorinate. A filter alone is not enough. Chemicals alone work poorly in cloudy water.

## Step 1: Clear cloudy water first

Dirt protects germs from heat, chlorine and sunlight. Clearing the water makes every other method work better.

1. Let the water stand in a container for 30 minutes or longer. Mud sinks to the bottom.
2. Pour the clear top part into a second container, slowly, without stirring up the bottom.
3. Pour it through a clean cotton cloth folded 4 to 8 times. A folded old sari, t-shirt, or a coffee filter all work.

> [!TIP]
> Filtering through cloth folded 8 times removes most of the tiny animals that carry cholera. In villages in Bangladesh, this simple step cut cholera cases by almost half.

For a better filter you can build yourself, see [Building a Sand and Charcoal Filter](../water/sand-filter.md).

## Step 2: Kill the germs

### Boiling (most reliable)

1. Bring the water to a **rolling boil**: big bubbles that keep coming even when you stir.
2. Keep it boiling for **1 minute**. At high altitude (above 2,000 m or 6,500 ft) boil for **3 minutes**, because water boils at a lower temperature there.
3. Let it cool in the covered pot. Do not add ice unless the ice was made from safe water.
4. To improve the flat taste, pour it back and forth between two clean containers.

> [!TIP]
> Fuel is precious. Once the water reaches a rolling boil, it is safe. Boiling for 10 or 20 minutes wastes fuel and gives no extra safety. To save fuel, see [Cooking Without Power](../fire/cook-without-power.md).

### Household bleach (chlorine)

Use **plain, unscented** bleach that contains **5-9% sodium hypochlorite** (check the label). Do not use bleach that is scented, "color-safe", "no-splash", or has added cleaners. Do not use bleach that is more than 1 year old if you can avoid it, because it weakens over time.

| Water | 5-6% bleach | 8.25% bleach |
|---|---|---|
| 1 liter (1 quart) | 2 drops | 2 drops |
| 4 liters (1 gallon) | 8 drops | 6 drops |
| 10 liters (2.5 gallons) | 20 drops (1/4 teaspoon) | 15 drops |
| 20 liters (5 gallons) | 40 drops (1/2 teaspoon) | 30 drops (1/3 teaspoon) |

1. Add the bleach to clear water. **Double the amount if the water is cloudy, colored or very cold.**
2. Stir or shake well.
3. Wait **30 minutes**.
4. Smell it. It should smell slightly of chlorine. If it does not, add the same dose again and wait another 15 minutes.

If you have no dropper, a clean straw works. Dip it 1 cm (half an inch) into the bleach, put a finger over the top and let drops fall one at a time.

> [!WARNING]
> Never drink bleach or add more than double the dose. Too much chlorine causes vomiting and burns. Keep bleach away from children. Never mix bleach with ammonia or acids (some toilet cleaners): it releases poison gas.

### Water purification tablets

Chlorine tablets (often sodium dichloroisocyanurate, NaDCC) and chlorine dioxide tablets are light and keep for years. Follow the packet exactly: the dose and waiting time differ between brands. Chlorine dioxide tablets also kill cryptosporidium if you wait the full time on the packet (often 4 hours).

### Iodine

1. Use **2% tincture of iodine** from a first aid kit.
2. Add **5 drops per liter** (quart) of clear water, or **10 drops** if it is cloudy or cold.
3. Wait **30 minutes**, or longer if the water is cold.

Do not use iodine if you are pregnant, have a thyroid problem, or are allergic to iodine. Do not use it every day for more than a few weeks.

### Sunlight (SODIS)

Ultraviolet light and heat from the sun kill most germs. This method is free and needs no fuel. It is recommended by the World Health Organization.

1. Use clean, **clear plastic (PET) bottles** of 2 liters or less. Remove labels. Do not use glass, colored or badly scratched bottles.
2. Only use **clear** water. Test: put the full bottle on top of large printed letters. If you can read them by looking down through the bottle, the water is clear enough.
3. Fill the bottle three-quarters full, close it, shake hard for 20 seconds, then fill it to the top.
4. Lay the bottle on its side in **full sun from morning to afternoon: at least 6 hours**. A metal roof or a sheet of metal underneath helps.
5. If more than half the sky is cloudy, leave it for **2 full days**.
6. Drink directly from the bottle. Do not pour it into dirty containers.

![Sunlight (SODIS): 1 clear plastic bottles of 2 liters or less, labels removed, filled to the top, closed and lying on their side. 2 metal sheet or metal roof underneath. 3 full sun from morning to afternoon, at least 6 hours, or 2 full days if more than half the sky is cloudy.](../../images/sodis.svg)

Do not rely on SODIS during continuous rain. Collect rainwater instead (see [Collecting Rainwater](../water/rainwater.md)).

### Filters

- **Commercial filters** with a pore size of 0.1-0.2 microns (straw filters, squeeze filters, ceramic filters) remove bacteria and parasites but **not viruses**. In a disaster where sewage has mixed with the water, also boil or chlorinate.
- **Home-made cloth, sand and charcoal filters** remove dirt and some germs. They make water clearer and better tasting. They do **not** make water safe on their own. Always disinfect afterwards.

## Salt water and chemical water

- **Seawater:** do not drink it, not even mixed with fresh water. It makes dehydration worse. Only distillation (turning water into steam and collecting the steam) makes seawater drinkable. See [Finding Water](../water/find-water.md).
- **Chemical contamination** (fuel, pesticides, industrial waste, heavy metals): boiling, bleach, SODIS and most filters do not help. Boiling can even make some chemicals stronger by removing water. Find another source. A filter that contains activated carbon reduces some chemicals but does not remove all of them.

## Keeping treated water safe

- Use containers with a tight lid and a narrow opening or a tap.
- Clean containers first with a mix of 1 teaspoon (5 mL) bleach per liter of water. Shake, empty, let air dry.
- Label the container with the date. Treated water stored this way stays safe for weeks or months. Re-treat it if it has been opened often or smells bad.
- Keep drinking water away from fuel, chemicals and toilets.

For storage amounts and how long it keeps, see [Storing Water](../water/store-water.md).

## Common mistakes and myths

- **"Running water is clean."** It is not. Streams carry germs from animals and people upstream.
- **"Water from a deep well is always safe."** Not after floods or earthquakes. Treat it.
- **"Alcohol or vinegar makes water safe."** They do not, at any amount you could drink.
- **"Boiling removes everything."** It kills germs. It does not remove chemicals, salt or metals.
- **"A few sips can't hurt."** One mouthful of contaminated water can cause cholera or typhoid.
- **Treating water but not the cup.** Clean the cup, the bottle and your hands too. See [Handwashing and Personal Hygiene](../hygiene/handwashing.md).

## If someone gets diarrhea

Diarrhea from bad water kills by dehydration, mostly children. Start oral rehydration solution immediately. See [Dehydration, Diarrhea and Oral Rehydration](../medical/dehydration.md).
