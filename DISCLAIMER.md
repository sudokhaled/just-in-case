# Disclaimer

**Just In Case is not a substitute for professional medical care, emergency services, or official instructions from local authorities.**

- **When professional help is available, use it.** Call emergency services, go to a clinic, and follow official evacuation and public health instructions. They know local conditions that this library cannot.
- **When help is not available,** this library tries to give the best current knowledge, checked against the sources listed in each article. It may still contain errors. Many articles are drafts that have not yet been reviewed by qualified experts. Each article shows its review status.
- **Medical information** is general guidance for emergencies. It is not a diagnosis or a treatment plan for any individual. Medicines, doses and procedures can harm people when used wrongly. Follow medicine packaging and professional advice whenever you can.
- **Wild food:** never eat a wild plant you have not identified with complete certainty. Never eat wild mushrooms. Some deadly plants look almost exactly like edible ones.
- **Laws differ** between countries, for example on trapping, fishing, foraging, radio transmission, building, and the use of firearms and chemicals. Know the law where you live.
- **You use this information at your own risk.** The contributors and maintainers accept no liability for any injury, loss or damage that results from using it.
