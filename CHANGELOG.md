## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-02

First public preview.

### Library

- 105 articles in 16 categories: survival priorities, water, fire, shelter, living on the land, food, growing food, first aid and medicine, hygiene, disasters, navigation and rescue, communication, power, skills, community, and reference. 27 are marked critical.
- Every article puts the deadly mistakes first, then quick steps, then details and sources.
- 38 line drawings with numbers instead of words, so they never need translating.
- 16 pocket cards for the most urgent procedures.

### Editions

- One-file web page with offline search, for any phone or computer.
- Website that installs as an offline app.
- EPUB for e-readers, and AZW3 for Kindle in the release downloads.
- UTF-8 text, plain ASCII core edition, and a short edition for MP3 players with one small file per topic.
- Printable A4 books with page numbers and an index, an A6 pocket booklet, and pocket cards.
- Each edition is cut to fit its device and checked against a size limit by the build.

### Hardware

- ESP32 and ESP8266 WiFi hotspot firmware that serves the whole library to nearby phones, with a one-click browser installer.
- Small-board edition for boards with about 0.5 MB of file space, such as the ESP-01.
- Raspberry Pi hotspot with an install script.

### Known limits

- No article has been reviewed by an expert yet. Each article shows its review status.
- English only. The build is ready for translations.
- The hotspot firmware is compiled and checked in CI, but has not yet been tested widely on real boards. Please report how it works on yours.
